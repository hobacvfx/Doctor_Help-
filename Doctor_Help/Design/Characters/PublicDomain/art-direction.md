# NPC art direction

Use the original NPC_0 prefab and original Head_Male, Eye_03, Mouth_04, Hair_03 and four original clothing sprites as the reference. Discard the first realistic costume studies; do not use generated Dracula as a style reference.

- Slight three-quarter view facing screen left, visible ear on screen right. Keep original anatomy and animation anchors; shift facial parts to the original left-facing positions rather than drawing a symmetrical frontal face.
- Thick uneven black outlines, occasional broken/scribbled contours, simple expressive shapes.
- Broad muted flat color blocks with rough black pencil contours and sparse short hatching in dark fold areas, under arms and behind lapels. Avoid perfectly clean vector contours; also avoid dense charcoal grain, fabric weave, realistic skin, embroidery or tiny folds.
- Large rounded head and slender angular neck, original torso and skeleton proportions.
- Reuse the existing project heads for ordinary characters: Frankenstein, Sherlock, Jekyll/Hyde, Medusa and Dracula. Only necessary special cases use separate head imagery: bandages, skull, pumpkin and wooden puppet. Hair/headwear, eye pair, nose, mouth and torso outfit remain separate sprites. Keep a genuine transparent neck opening.
- High collars are separate rear-layer sprites behind both Neck and Head; never squeeze anatomy to fit clothing.
- Garment bodies and sleeves extend past the underlying body and cover its sides; test coverage on the unchanged NPC_0 anatomy.

The flat sprite sheets are the accepted production direction. Files named *_Source without _Flat are discarded studies retained only during production, not referenced by NPCs.
