- **Brain Rot** (Bệnh thối não): Brain Abnormality (Tung Tung Tung), Slow Heartbeat, Headache → Science Book.
- **Brittle Bone Disease** (Bệnh xương thủy tinh): Bone Fracture, High Temperature → Calcium.
- **Zombie Infection**: Worm Infestation, High Temperature, Nausea → Report to Police.
- **Vampire Infection**: Heart Abnormality, No Heartbeat, Low Temperature → Report to Police.

There are 12 diseases, 15 unique symptoms, and 9 unique remedies. Science Book is in PillProfile as directed by the diagram. Report to Police is a treatment mode, not a pill.
Brain Abnormality (Tung Tung Tung) is preserved as a distinct symptom because the diagram explicitly qualifies it differently from Brain Abnormality.
All listed pills are required together. The current diagram assigns one remedy per medication-treated disease; the data model also supports multiple required pills.
These assets are editable design data. Do not regenerate them on startup or reset IDs when changing display names.
